Privacy Policy

Version of 13 November 2026

Who is responsible for your data

The Wattermelon app and its owner ("we"). For any question about your data, write to hello@wattermelon.com.

What data we process

  • Account: email and password. The password is stored only as a hash by the Supabase authentication service.
  • Profile: name, city, sex, year of birth, height, weight, FTP, food preferences, active training plan.
  • Profile photo - if you add one. Only signed-in users see it next to your invites and messages in Together; it cannot be opened without signing in.
  • Workouts from Garmin Connect - only if you connect Garmin yourself: date, distance, time, power, heart rate, calories, training load.
  • Cycling workouts from Apple Health - only if you grant access yourself: date, time, distance, heart rate, power, calories. We only read this data, never write anything to Health and never use it for advertising.
  • Rides from intervals.icu and from FIT files you upload - the same ride metrics.
  • Food diary and meal swaps in your menu - stored only on your phone.
  • Your intervals.icu key - only in your phone's Keychain.
  • Location - only while you use the app and only to find people to ride with. A ride invite stores coordinates rounded to about 1 km and the name of the area; other users see only the area and an approximate distance. Your name and sex from your profile are shown with the invite.
  • Ride invites (when, roughly where, pace, meeting point and its spot on the map), replies using preset phrases, suggested meeting points, reports and blocks.
  • Technical records of failed saves: the type of error, its text and the app version - so we can find and fix problems faster.

Why and on what legal basis

  • Sign-in, sync between devices and running the app's features - performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Weight, heart rate, nutrition and workouts are health data. We process them only with your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR) to calculate training load, fitness and your nutrition targets.
  • Location - with your consent, which you can withdraw in your iPhone settings.
  • Handling reports and preventing abuse - our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Where data is stored

In a Supabase database in an EU data centre (Ireland). Data is transferred only over a secure connection (HTTPS). You have access to your data; a coach has access only if you grant it yourself, and you can revoke it at any time.

Who we share data with

We do not sell data, do not show ads and do not track you across other apps. Data goes to third-party services only at your command: Garmin Connect and intervals.icu (importing rides and sending workouts to your device). Address search for meeting points and area names use Apple Maps. These services have their own privacy policies.

How long we keep data

For as long as you have an account. Ride invites and their messages are deleted one week after the ride time. Reports are kept until they are reviewed and for 90 days after that; error records for 30 days. Once a day we make an encrypted backup of the database and keep it for up to 90 days: when you delete your account, your data disappears from the app and the database right away, and from backups within 90 days.

Your rights

You can get a copy of your data, correct it, move it, restrict processing or withdraw consent. Write to hello@wattermelon.com - we reply within one month. You can delete your account and all your data right away in the app: Profile › Delete account. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority.

Age

The app is only for people aged 18 and over.
