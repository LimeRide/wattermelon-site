About the app

Wattermelon is a cycling training companion: training load, plans, nutrition and finding people to ride with. Its advice and calculations are guidelines for a healthy adult athlete, not medical advice. If you have an illness, injury or special diet, see a doctor.

Account

Give accurate information and do not share your password. You must be at least 18 years old. You can delete your account at any time in your profile.

Riding together

This feature is for people aged 18 and over. We do not verify users' identities and are not responsible for their behaviour: you decide whether to ride with someone.

  • Meet in public places and tell someone close to you who you are riding with and where.
  • Follow traffic rules, wear a helmet and choose a pace you can handle.
  • Do not post your home address or personal details in an invite. The meeting point and its spot on the map are visible only to people who reply.
  • Messaging works only with preset phrases and meeting point suggestions. Links, emails and phone numbers are not allowed in invites or meeting points.
  • Limits: no more than 3 active invites and 5 per day, 20 messages per hour, 5 reports per day.

What is not allowed

Insults, threats, harassment, discrimination, spam and advertising, illegal or sexual content, posting other people's personal data, impersonating someone else. We have zero tolerance for this.

Reports and blocking

You can report any invite or message and block its author: you will stop seeing each other. We review reports within 24 hours, remove violating content and close offenders' accounts. Invites reported by several people are hidden automatically.

Third-party services

Garmin Connect, Apple Health and intervals.icu are connected only if you choose to and work under their own rules. We are not responsible for their availability.

Liability

The app is provided "as is". Cycling involves risk - you ride at your own risk. To the extent permitted by law, we are not liable for damage resulting from using the app or meeting other users.
